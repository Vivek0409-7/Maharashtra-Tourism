ABOUT BHIMASHANKAR

A Sacred Journey Into The Sahyadri

Bhimashankar is a famous pilgrimage destination situated in the Sahyadri ranges of Maharashtra.

It is known for the sacred Bhimashankar Temple, one of the twelve Jyotirlingas dedicated to Lord Shiva.

The surrounding forests and wildlife make Bhimashankar a unique combination of spirituality, nature and adventure.

Why is Bhimashankar Important?

Bhimashankar is one of the twelve Jyotirlingas dedicated to Lord Shiva.

The temple is an important pilgrimage destination and is surrounded by the Bhimashankar Wildlife Sanctuary.

The temple showcases traditional Indian architecture with Nagara-style elements and detailed stone carvings.

The region combines spirituality, history and biodiversity, making it a unique destination.
