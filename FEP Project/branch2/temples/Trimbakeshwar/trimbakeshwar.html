ABOUT TRIMBAKESHWAR

A Sacred Origin Beneath Brahmagiri Hills

Trimbakeshwar is an ancient Hindu pilgrim center located at the foot of the scenic Brahmagiri Hills near Nashik, Maharashtra.

It houses one of the twelve sacred Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva. Uniquely, the Linga here features three faces representing Lord Brahma, Lord Vishnu, and Lord Shiva.

It is also revered as the birthplace of the holy river Godavari, blending deep spiritual heritage with picturesque natural surroundings.

Why is Trimbakeshwar Important?

Trimbakeshwar is unique among all twelve Jyotirlingas because its linga embodies the three supreme deities: Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva.

Built completely out of black basalt stone by Peshwa Balaji Baji Rao in the 18th century, it showcases exemplary Hemadpanthi architecture.

The temple sanctuary is closely associated with Maharshi Gautama, who performed penance here to bring the river Godavari down to earth.

It serves as a primary site for sacred Vedic rituals such as Kalsarp Dosh Nivaran and Narayan Nagbali ceremonies.

How To Reach Trimbakeshwar

🚗

By Road

Located around 28 km from Nashik city, well connected via smooth state highways.

🚌

By Bus

Frequent MSRTC buses and private taxis run from Nashik CBS bus stand directly to Trimbak.

🚆

By Railway

Nashik Road Railway Station (NK) is the nearest major railway junction (around 36 km away).

✈

By Air

Nashik Airport (Ozar) is ~50 km away, and Mumbai Chhatrapati Shivaji Airport is ~170 km away.
