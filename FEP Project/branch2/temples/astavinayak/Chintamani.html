About Chintamani Mandir

Chintamani at Theur is revered as the remover of anxieties and worries[cite: 4]. Situated near the confluence of three rivers—Mula, Mutha, and Bhima—this temple was heavily patronised by the Peshwas[cite: 4].

Madhavrao Peshwa spent his final days in the calm premises of this shrine, constructing the spacious Sabhamandap (main wooden assembly hall).

📜 Mythological Legend

Sage Kapila possessed the wish-fulfilling jewel Chintamani, which was stolen by the greedy Prince Gana. Lord Ganesha retrieved the precious gem under a Kadamba tree here[cite: 4], earning the title Chintamani.

🛕 Unique Wooden Sabhamandap

The temple hall is constructed out of fine wood, showcasing classic Maratha architecture and traditional Peshwa woodwork carvings.
