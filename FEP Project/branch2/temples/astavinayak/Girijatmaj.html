Girijatmaj Temple (Lenyadri)

About Girijatmaj Mandir

Girijatmaj ('Son of Girija/Parvati') at Lenyadri is unique as the only Ashtavinayak shrine situated inside an ancient rock-cut cave (Cave 18)[cite: 6].

Reached by climbing ~307 stone steps carved into the mountain side[cite: 6], the cave hall features monolithic pillars and zero electrical artificial lighting inside the main sanctum.

LocationLenyadri, Junnar, Pune
Highlight307 Steps Rock Cave
Cave SiteCave 18 (Buddhist Caves)
Next Stop~140 km to Pali

📜 Mythological Legend

Goddess Parvati (Girija) performed penance for 12 years inside these caves to manifest Lord Ganesha as her son[cite: 6]. Ganesha lived here during his childhood days.

🧗 Visitor Advisory

Climbing requires moderate physical fitness (~307 steps)[cite: 6]. Palanquin (Doli) services are available for elderly devotees at the base hill.
