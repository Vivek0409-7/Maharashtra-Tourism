About Mayureshwar Mandir

Mayureshwar (or Moreshwar) in Morgaon marks the traditional beginning and completion point of the Ashtavinayak Yatra[cite: 8]. Situated along the Karha river, the town historically resembles the shape of a peacock[cite: 8].

Constructed from black stone during the Bahmani era, the temple architecture resembles a fortress complete with four tall minaret-like pillars at each corner and high enclosure walls[cite: 8].

📜 Mythological Significance

According to the Ganesha Purana, Lord Ganesha incarnated as Mayureshwar (riding a peacock, or Mayura) to defeat the demon Sindhu[cite: 8]. After an intense battle, Ganesha vanquished the demon at this very spot[cite: 8].

An unusual feature of this temple is a large stone sculpture of Nandi (Lord Shiva's mount) seated directly outside the main entrance facing Ganesha[cite: 8].

🛕 Key Timings & Pilgrimage Guidance

Temple Opening Hours: 5:00 AM – 10:00 PM

Pooja Timings: Morning Kakad Aarti at 5:30 AM, Madhyanha Pooja at 12:00 PM, and Shej Aarti at 10:00 PM.

Pilgrimage Rule: As per tradition, pilgrims begin their Ashtavinayak Yatra at Morgaon, visit all 8 shrines, and return here to complete the circuit.
