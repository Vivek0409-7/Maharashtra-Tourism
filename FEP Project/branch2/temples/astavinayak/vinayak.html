About Varadvinayak Mandir

Varadvinayak at Mahad is celebrated as the bestower of boons (Varada). It is renowned for being one of the few shrines where devotees can enter the inner sanctum directly to offer prayers.

A sacred oil lamp (Nandadeep) inside the sanctum has been continuously burning uninterrupted since 1892.

📜 Mythological Heritage

Prince Rukmangada prayed to Ganesha here to relieve a curse. Pleased with his devotion, Ganesha granted his boon, establishing the shrine as Varadvinayak (Giver of Boons).

🛕 Temple Surroundings

The shrine features tiled roofs, ancient stone carvings of elephant guards, and a peaceful pond (Pushkarani) located right beside the shrine.
