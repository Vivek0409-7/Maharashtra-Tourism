About Vighneshwar Mandir

Vighneshwar at Ozar is situated along the banks of the Kukadi River. Enclosed by thick stone fort-like walls, its golden dome (Shikhara) shines brightly under the sun.

The idol features precious rubies embedded into its eyes and a diamond on its forehead, giving it a radiant aura.

📜 Mythological History

When King Abhinandan attempted a sacrifice, Indra sent demon Vighnasura to create obstacles. Sage Parashar prayed to Ganesha, who defeated the demon. Vighnasura surrendered and requested that Ganesha take his name as 'Vighneshwar' (Lord of Obstacles).

🛕 Architectural Features

The temple is surrounded by a wide paved stone courtyard, Deepmalas (lamp towers), and spacious inner chambers.
