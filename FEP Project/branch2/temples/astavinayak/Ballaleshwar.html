About Ballaleshwar Mandir

Ballaleshwar at Pali is historically significant as the only Ashtavinayak shrine named directly after a devotee—a young boy named Ballal[cite: 3].

Nestled between the Sarasgad fort hill and Amba River, the east-facing temple sanctum allows morning sunlight to directly fall upon the deity during sunrise[cite: 3].

📜 Devotion of Young Ballal

Boy Ballal's intense devotion to Ganesha angered his father, who beat him and tied him to a tree. Impressed by the boy's steadfast prayers, Lord Ganesha appeared, blessed him, and chose to reside here in his name[cite: 3].

🛕 Historic Portuguese Bell

The temple houses a massive bell brought by Chimaji Appa after his victory over the Portuguese at Vasai Fort.
