About Siddhivinayak Mandir

Siddhivinayak at Siddhatek sits on a hilltop along the pristine banks of the Bhima River[cite: 10]. It is celebrated as the bestower of spiritual accomplishment (Siddhi)[cite: 10].

It stands unique among all eight shrines because Ganesha's trunk is turned to the right (Right-trunked / Siddhi-Vinayak)[cite: 10], requiring strict spiritual discipline during prayers.

📜 History & Legend

Mythology holds that Lord Vishnu invoked Lord Ganesha here before vanquishing demons Madhu and Kaitabha. After gaining Siddhi, Vishnu constructed this sanctum on top of the hill.

The temple core was later renovated during the reign of Ahilyabai Holkar, who constructed the main inner stone sanctum.

🛕 Pradakshina Rituals

Devotees perform a 1-kilometer circumambulation (Pradakshina) around the entire hill housing the temple to seek special blessings.
