About Mahaganapati Mandir

Mahaganapati at Ranjangaon represents Ganesha's most powerful, supreme form[cite: 5]. Lore describes this idol as having ten trunks and twenty arms (Mahotkata form)[cite: 5].

Facing east, the temple entrance is designed so that during solar transitions, morning sunlight illuminates the idol directly[cite: 5].

📜 Divine Legend

Lord Shiva prayed to Mahaganapati here before waging battle against the demon Tripurasura[cite: 5]. After securing victory, Shiva consecrated the original shrine, giving the town the name Ranjangaon (the town of delight)[cite: 5].

🛕 Temple Structure

Built by Madhavrao Peshwa, the temple features huge stone gateway pillars (Minarets) resembling Maratha fortifications.
