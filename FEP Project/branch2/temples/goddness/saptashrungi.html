ABODE OF SEVEN PEAKS

The Rock Carved Goddess of Sahyadri

Shree Saptashrungi Nivasini Devi resides on a high cliff surrounded by seven mountain peaks ('Sapta-Shrunga') in the Western Ghats of Nashik district.

The deity idol is eight feet tall and carved directly onto the natural rock wall, featuring eighteen arms holding weapons granted by celestial gods.

Devotees traditionally ascend roughly 500 stone steps to reach the hilltop sanctum, or take the modern funicular ropeway system.

Saptashrungi Temple Schedule

🏔️

Darshan Guidelines

Mountain Timing: Temple doors open at 5:00 AM daily. Ropeway service operates continuous round trips.

Sindoor Application: The idol is coated with natural red vermillion (Sindoor) during special periodic rituals.

TRAVEL GUIDE

How to Reach Saptashrungi

🚗

By Road

Well connected asphalt roads via Nashik - Dindori - Vani route.

🚌

By Bus

Frequent MSRTC buses ply continuously from Nashik CBS bus stand directly to hill base.

🚆

By Rail

Nashik Road Railway Station (65 km) is connected to all major Indian cities.

✈️

By Air

Nashik Ozar Airport (ISK) is nearest (45 km) with direct flights from major cities.
