THE LEGEND OF KARVEER

Eternal Abode of Goddess Ambabai

The Shri Mahalakshmi Temple of Kolhapur is one of the premier Shakti Peethas in India. According to ancient scriptures, Lord Vishnu and Goddess Lakshmi reside permanently in this holy land known as Karveer Kshetra.

Built in the 7th century during the Chalukya dynasty, the temple features intricate Hemadpanti architecture. The idol is crafted from gemstone and weighs roughly 40 kg, adorned with precious jewels during daily pujas.

A famous annual natural phenomenon named Kirnotsav occurs when the rays of the setting sun fall directly on the deity's feet, waist, and face twice a year.

Darshan Guidelines

Mukh Darshan: Continuous queue line available throughout the day from morning until closing.

Abhishek Pujas: Performed early morning between 8:00 AM and 9:30 AM with advance booking options.

Special Dress Code: Traditional attire is encouraged when entering inner sanctums for special pujas.

TRAVEL GUIDE

How to Reach Kolhapur

🚗

By Road

Direct highway connection via Mumbai-Bengaluru Highway (NH 48).

🚌

By Bus

Direct MSRTC Shivshahi and private luxury buses run daily from Pune, Mumbai, and Goa.

🚆

By Rail

Shri Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Terminus (Kolhapur Railway Station) connects major cities.

✈️

By Air

Kolhapur Airport (KLH) has direct daily flights connecting to Hyderabad, Tirupati, and Mumbai.
