PROTECTRESS OF SWARAJ

The Divine Force of Tuljapur

Goddess Tulja Bhavani is considered a Swayambhu (self-manifested) idol in Tuljapur. She is revered as the family deity (Kuldaiwat) of millions across Maharashtra and neighboring states.

Legend tells that Goddess Bhavani presented the sacred 'Bhavani Talwar' (sword) to Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, blessing him with valor and victory.

The temple is accessed via dynamic stone steps leading down to Yamuna and Kallola holy water tanks inside the stone complex.

TRAVEL GUIDE

How to Reach Tuljapur

🚗

By Road

Situated on NH 52 connecting Solapur and Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar.

🚌

By Bus

Direct MSRTC buses connect directly from Solapur (45 km), Pune, and Mumbai.

🚆

By Rail

Solapur Railway Station (45 km) is the nearest major railway junction.

✈️

By Air

Pune Airport (290 km) or Hyderabad Airport (300 km) serve as nearest air hubs.
