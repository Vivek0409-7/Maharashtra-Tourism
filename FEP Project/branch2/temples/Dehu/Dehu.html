DISCOVER DEHU

Sant Tukaram Maharaj Temple

Birthplace & Abhang Bhumi of Sant Tukaram

Dehu is a historic pilgrimage town located on the banks of the Indrayani River in Pune district, Maharashtra.

It is the birthplace of the revered 17th-century Warkari saint Sant Tukaram Maharaj, famed for his timeless Abhangas and devotion to Lord Vitthal.

Every year during Tukaram Beej and the Ashadhi Palkhi procession, thousands of Warkari pilgrims flock to Dehu to pay homage and sing devotional kirtans.

Visitors can experience the iconic Gatha Mandir, the historic Old Dehu Temple, Indrayani River Ghats, and Bhandara Hill nearby.

How To Reach Dehu

🚌

By Bus

Direct PMPML buses connect Dehu to Nigdi, Pimpri, Swargate, and Pune Station.

PMPML Bus / Local Cabs
🚆

By Train

Dehu Road Railway Station is nearby, and Pune Junction (approx. 27 km) offers major connections.

Dehu Road / Pune Junction Railway Station
✈️

By Air

Pune International Airport (PNQ) at Lohegaon is approximately 30 km from Dehu.

Pune Airport (PNQ)

💡 Helpful Travel Tips

  • Visit Gatha Mandir early in the morning for a peaceful environment and brief lines.
  • Combine your trip with a visit to Bhandara Hill for meditation and panoramic views.
  • Expect heavy footfalls during Tukaram Beej; plan local transport in advance.
  • Store your footwear safely at official counter stands before entering temple areas.
