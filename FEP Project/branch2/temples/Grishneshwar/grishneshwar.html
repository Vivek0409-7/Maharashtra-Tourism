ABOUT GRISHNESHWAR

A Sacred Realm of Heritage & Faith

Grishneshwar, also known as Ghrushneshwar, is an ancient pilgrimage shrine located near Ellora in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar district.

It is revered as the 12th and final Jyotirlinga shrine dedicated to Lord Shiva mentioned in the Shiva Purana.

Rebuilt in red basalt stone by Ahilyabai Holkar, the temple displays breathtaking South-Indian and Hemadpanthi style architecture.

Why is Grishneshwar Important?

Grishneshwar holds great religious significance as the last of the 12 sacred Jyotirlingas in India.

The original structure was destroyed during medieval conflicts and later restored to its grand glory by Maharani Ahilyabai Holkar of Indore in the 18th century.

Built with red rocks, the temple spire features detailed carvings of Hindu gods and traditional courtly motifs.

Its location right next to Ellora Caves connects spiritual pilgrims directly with ancient Indian rock-cut craft.

How To Reach Grishneshwar

🚗

By Road

Located ~30 km from Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar via well-connected highway routes.

🚌

By Bus

Frequent MSRTC state buses and private taxis ply regularly between Sambhajinagar and Ellora.

🚆

By Railway

Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar Railway Station (AWB) is the primary rail link (~30 km away).

✈

By Air

Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar Airport (IXU) connects directly with domestic routes across India.
