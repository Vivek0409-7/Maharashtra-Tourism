DISCOVER SHEGAON

Shri Gajanan Maharaj Temple

A Sacred Pilgrimage Town

Shegaon is a holy pilgrimage town in Buldhana district, Maharashtra, famous for Sant Shri Gajanan Maharaj.

The Shri Gajanan Maharaj Mandir complex is renowned across India for its immaculate cleanliness, organization, and spiritual atmosphere.

The town features remarkable attractions like Anand Sagar, a sprawling garden and spiritual complex maintained by the Sansthan.

Visitors can easily access comfortable lodging, clean dining facilities, and seamless local transport provided by the temple trust.
