DISCOVER PANDHARPUR

Shri Vitthal Rukmini Temple

A Sacred Pilgrimage

Pandharpur is a celebrated holy city in Solapur district, Maharashtra, revered as the abode of Lord Vitthal and His consort Goddess Rukmini.

The Shri Vitthal Rukmini Mandir is the center of the Warkari sect, welcoming millions of devotees during the annual Ashadhi and Kartiki Wavari yatras.

The temple complex features historic ghats along the sacred Chandrabhaga River, along with several shrines dedicated to saint-poets like Dnyaneshwar, Tukaram, and Namdev.

Apart from spiritual attractions, visitors can find accommodations, local Maharashtrian dining options, and convenient transit links around the town.

Important Visitor Information

💡 Helpful Travel Tips

  • Plan ahead if traveling during Ashadhi or Kartiki Ekadashi due to large crowds.
  • Book Mukh Darshan e-passes on the official website to save waiting time.
  • Visit Chandrabhaga River during early morning hours for peaceful prayers.
  • Keep personal belongings secure and wear comfortable shoes.
