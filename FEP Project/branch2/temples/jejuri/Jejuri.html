DISCOVER JEJURI

Lord Khandoba Temple

The Sacred Land of Yelkot Yelkot Jai Malhar

Jejuri is a renowned pilgrimage town in Pune district, Maharashtra, famous for the hilltop temple dedicated to Lord Khandoba, an incarnation of Lord Shiva.

Popularly known as Sonepuri (Golden City), the temple and hill turn golden yellow as devotees scatter turmeric powder (Bhandara) in reverence to the deity.

Millions of devotees visit Jejuri during Somvati Amavasya and Bhandara festivals to witness the unique ritual of turmeric shower and seek blessings.

Visitors can climb the historic stone steps (Deepmalas), explore the fort-like temple architecture, and visit nearby spots like Lavthaleshwar and Kadapepathar.

Important Visitor Information

During Somvati Amavasya, Champa Sashti, and major Bhandara festivals, the temple remains open extended hours. Heavy crowds are expected, so plan your visit accordingly.

Pune Airport (PNQ)

💡 Helpful Travel Tips

  • Wear comfortable walking shoes to climb the ~350 stone steps to the main fort temple.
  • Protect your eyes and phone cameras from airborne turmeric powder (Bhandara) on festival days.
  • Visit early morning or late evening during summers to avoid hot afternoon stone steps.
  • Palanquin (Palkhi) services are available at the base for elderly or needy devotees.
