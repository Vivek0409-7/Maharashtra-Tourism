DISCOVER SHIRDI

Shri Sai Baba Temple

A Sacred Destination

Shirdi is a renowned pilgrimage town in Maharashtra associated with the life and teachings of Sai Baba.

The Shri Sai Baba Samadhi Mandir is the central attraction of Shirdi and welcomes devotees throughout the year.

The temple complex and surrounding area contain several places connected with Sai Baba's life, including Dwarkamai, Chavadi and Lendi Baug.

Apart from spiritual attractions, visitors can find hotels, restaurants, shopping areas and transportation facilities around the temple.
