DISCOVER ALANDI

Sant Dnyaneshwar Maharaj Temple

A Sacred Warkari Pilgrim Town

Alandi is a revered pilgrimage site situated on the banks of the sacred Indrayani River in Pune district, Maharashtra.

The temple marks the Samadhi Sanjeevan site of Saint Dnyaneshwar Maharaj, the renowned 13th-century saint and author of Dnyaneshwari.

Every year, millions of Warkari pilgrims visit Alandi and participate in the historic Ashadhi Ekadashi Palkhi procession to Pandharpur.

Visitors can explore various ghats, historical mathas, lodging, and religious shopping areas surrounding the temple complex.

TRAVEL GUIDE

How To Reach Alandi

🚌

By Bus

Frequent PMPML buses connect Alandi with Pune Station, Swargate, and Pimpri-Chinchwad.

Pune City Bus / Auto
🚆

By Train

Pune Junction (approx. 22 km) and Kasarwadi/Pimpri stations are the nearest rail points.

Pune Junction Railway Station
✈️

By Air

Pune International Airport (PNQ) at Lohegaon is just around 15 km away from Alandi.

Pune Airport (PNQ)

💡 Helpful Travel Tips

  • Visit early in the morning to avoid long darshan queue wait times.
  • Take time to sit quietly along the Indrayani River ghats in the evening.
  • Plan carefully during Kartiki Ekadashi as the town gets extremely crowded.
  • Keep footwear safely at designated deposit counters before entry.
