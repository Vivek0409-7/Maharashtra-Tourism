Spiritual Sites in Maharashtra

Explore the land of saints, ancient temples, and divine energy

Maharashtra features a deeply rooted spiritual landscape, welcoming millions of seekers, pilgrims, and travelers every year. Use the search bar above to find specific shrines quickly.

The Sacred Jyotirlingas

Nashik District

Trimbakeshwar

Famous for its unique three-faced linga representing Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva, this holy site is situated right near the origin of the sacred Godavari River.

Pune District

Bhimashankar

Tucked away inside deep Sahyadri forests and a wildlife reserve, this shrine combines dense nature with immense spiritual reverence for Lord Shiva.

Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar District

Grishneshwar

Located right next to the historic Ellora Caves, this ancient structure is recognized traditionally as the 12th and last Jyotirlinga on earth.

Devotional & Saint Shrines

Ahilyanagar District

Shirdi

A globally renowned spiritual hub dedicated to the revered 20th-century saint Sri Sai Baba, featuring the popular Samadhi Mandir and Dwarkamai.

Solapur District

Pandharpur

The spiritual heartbeat of Maharashtra, housing Lord Vitthal and Rukmini. It serves as the final destination for the legendary annual Wari pilgrimage.

Buldhana District

Shegaon

The peaceful resting place of Sant Gajanan Maharaj, known nationwide for its immaculate cleanliness, lush gardens, and tranquil atmosphere.

Pune District

Alandi

The spiritual heartbeat of Maharashtra. The final resting places (Samadhis) of Maharashtra’s most revered poet-saints, Sant Dnyaneshwar (Alandi).

Pune District

Dehu

The spiritual heartbeat of Maharashtra. The final resting places (Samadhis) of Maharashtra’s most revered poet-saints, Sant Tukaram (Dehu).

Jejuri, Pune District

Khandoba Temple

The Golden Temple (Sonyachi Jejuri). Dedicated to Lord Khandoba . Renowned for the Bhandara tradition, where devotees shower turmeric powder across the entire fort-like temple campus. Built atop a hill with over 300 steps and iconic Deepmalas.

Shakti Peethas & Goddess Temples

Kolhapur

Mahalakshmi Temple

One of the prominent Shakti Peethas in India, where devotees gather to seek the blessings of Goddess Mahalakshmi (Ambabai) with immense devotion.

Dharashiv (Tuljapur)

Tulja Bhavani Temple

A historic temple dedicated to Goddess Tulja Bhavani, who has historically been revered as the protective patron deity of the Maratha empire.

Nashik District

Saptashrungi

A breathtaking mountain temple perched high across seven natural mountain peaks, dedicated to the Goddess incarnation manifested with eighteen arms.

The Ashtavinayak (meaning "eight Grianeshwar/Ganesha idols")

Morgaon, Pune District

Mayureshwar Temple

The starting and ending point of the yatra; features Ganesha riding a peacock (Mayura) with a unique Nandi statue outside.

Siddhatek, Ahmednagar District

Siddhivinayak Temple

Situated on the banks of the Bhima River; the only temple in the circuit where Ganesha's trunk turns to the right.

Pali, Raigad District

Ballaleshwari

The only Ashtavinayak temple named after a devout young boy and devotee, Ballal.

Mahad, Raigad District

Varadvinayak Temple

Giver of Boons. Known for fulfilling wishes (Varada). Features an eternal oil lamp (Nandadeep) burning continuously since 1892. Devotees can enter the main sanctum directly.

Theur, Pune District

Chintamani Temple

Remover of Worries. Ganesha recovered the precious jewel (Chintamani) for Sage Kapila here. Set at the confluence of the Mula, Mutha, and Bhima rivers.

Lenyadri, Pune District

Girijatmaj Temple

Son of Parvati. Situated inside Cave 18 of a Buddhist rock-cut complex atop a hill (~307 steps). Goddess Parvati (Girija) performed penance here to receive Ganesha as her son.

Ozar, Pune District

Vighneshwar Temple

Remover of Obstacles. Ganesha vanquished the demon of obstacles, Vighnasura, who surrendered at this location. Built inside fortified stone walls and features ruby-set eyes.

Ranjangaon, Pune District

Mahaganapati Temple

The Circuit End. Represents Ganesha's most powerful form with 10 trunks and 20 arms in lore. Lord Shiva prayed here prior to defeating Tripurasura. Completes the pilgrimage.
