Overview

is a historic hill fort located near Kolhapur in Maharashtra. Built on the Sahyadri mountain ranges, it is one of the largest and most important forts in the region. The fort was originally developed during the Shilahara period and later came under the control of various ruling dynasties, including the Bahamanis, Adilshahis, and Marathas.
Panhala Fort holds a special place in Maratha history because of its association with Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj. The fort played an important role in the Maratha struggle for power and defence. It is particularly famous for the escape of Shivaji Maharaj from Panhala during the siege of 1660, when Baji Prabhu Deshpande made a heroic stand at Ghodkhind, later known as Pavan Khind.
The fort is known for its massive stone walls, historic gates, ancient temples, and scenic views of the surrounding valleys. Important attractions include Sajja Kothi, Ambabai Temple, Teen Darwaza, and the historic fortification structures. Today, Panhala Fort is a popular destination for historical tourism, heritage exploration, and sightseeing.

Historical Highlights

Ancient Origins & Historical Importance

Panhala Fort was originally developed during the Shilahara period and later came under the control of various ruling dynasties. The fort’s strategic location near Kolhapur made it important for controlling trade routes and protecting the surrounding region. Its strong fortifications and elevated position helped it remain an important military centre throughout history.

Shivaji Maharaj & The Siege of Panhala

Panhala Fort is closely associated with Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj. In 1660, the fort was besieged by the forces of Adilshahi commander Siddi Jauhar. Shivaji Maharaj successfully escaped from Panhala during the siege and moved towards Vishalgad. This event became an important chapter in Maratha history and demonstrated the strategic importance of the fort.

Baji Prabhu Deshpande & Pavan Khind

During Shivaji Maharaj’s escape from Panhala, Baji Prabhu Deshpande and his troops held back the pursuing forces at Ghodkhind. Their courageous resistance allowed Shivaji Maharaj to reach Vishalgad safely. After the battle, Ghodkhind was renamed Pavan Khind in honour of this sacrifice. The event remains an important part of Maratha heritage.

Major Attractions

Attraction Description
Sajja Kothi A historic multi-storey structure associated with the fort’s administration and royal history. It offers beautiful views of the surrounding landscape.
Teen Darwaza A historic triple-arched entrance that reflects the fort’s defensive architecture and strategic planning.
Ambabai Temple An ancient temple dedicated to Goddess Ambabai, known for its religious and cultural importance.
Andhar Bavad A historic underground stepwell designed to provide access to water and protect the water supply during attacks.
Sambhaji Temple A religious structure associated with the fort’s historical and cultural heritage.
Kalyan Darwaza A historic entrance associated with the fort’s defensive architecture.
