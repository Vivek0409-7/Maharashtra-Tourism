Overview

Lohagad & Visapur Forts are historic hill forts located near Lonavala in the Pune district of Maharashtra. Situated in the Sahyadri Mountains, these forts are known for their rich Maratha heritage, ancient fortifications, and scenic mountain landscapes. Lohagad Fort, meaning “Iron Fort,” is famous for its strong stone walls, four historic gates, and the distinctive Vinchu Kata ridge. Visapur Fort, located nearby, is known for its massive plateau, rugged terrain, and extensive fortification ruins.
Both forts played important roles in the military history of the region. Lohagad is closely associated with Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj and the Maratha fort network, while Visapur was developed during the Peshwa period. Their strategic locations helped control important routes connecting the Deccan region with the Konkan coast.
Today, Lohagad and Visapur are popular destinations for trekking, heritage tourism, and nature exploration. Visitors can enjoy panoramic views of the Sahyadri Mountains, explore ancient structures, and experience the historical importance of these remarkable hill forts.

Historical Highlights

Ancient Origins & Maratha Heritage

Lohagad and Visapur Forts form part of the historic fort network of the Sahyadri Mountains. Lohagad has ancient origins and witnessed the rule of different dynasties before becoming an important Maratha stronghold. Visapur was developed during the early 18th century under Peshwa Balaji Vishwanath. Both forts reflect the military planning and architectural heritage of Maharashtra.

Shivaji Maharaj & Military Importance

Lohagad Fort became an important part of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj’s fort network during the 17th century. Its strategic location helped control important routes between the Deccan and Konkan regions. Visapur Fort was later developed as part of the Maratha defensive system and was strategically connected with nearby Lohagad. Together, the forts represent the importance of mountain fortifications in Maratha history.

Vinchu Kata & Scenic Fort Architecture

Lohagad is famous for Vinchu Kata, a long fortified ridge resembling a scorpion’s tail. Visapur is known for its massive plateau, ancient caves, and extensive fortification ruins. The stone walls, bastions, and elevated terrain of both forts reflect their defensive architecture. Their scenic landscapes make them popular destinations for trekking and heritage exploration.
