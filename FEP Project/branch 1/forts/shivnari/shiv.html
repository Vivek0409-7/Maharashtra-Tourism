Overview

Shivneri Fort is a historic military fortification located near Junnar in Pune district, Maharashtra, India. Perched on a massive hill at an elevation of over 1,000 meters (3,500 feet), it is internationally renowned as the birthplace of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, the founder of the Maratha Empire.

Historical Highlights

Birthplace of a Legend

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj was born here on February 19, 1630. He spent his early childhood within the secure walls of this fort.

Strategic Defenses

Built with seven heavy gates in a zigzag layout, the fort was engineered to be virtually impregnable to invading enemy forces.

Under Various Dynasties

Before coming under Maratha rule, the fort was governed by the Yadavas, the Bahshahi rulers, the Nizam Shahs, and the Mughals.

Major Attractions

Attraction Description
Shivai Devi Temple A revered temple dedicated to Goddess Shivai, after whom Shivaji Maharaj was named.
Birthplace Monument A dedicated memorial building housing statues of young Shivaji Maharaj and his mother, Jijabai.
Seven Fort Gates A series of robust, heavily fortified entry gates built sequentially up the hill slope.
Badami Talav A central water reservoir supplying fresh water to the entire fort complex.
Kopreshwar / Ganga-Jamuna Tanks Natural underground springs providing perennial water storage on top of the hill.

Essential Visitor Information

  • Location: Junnar, Pune District, Maharashtra
