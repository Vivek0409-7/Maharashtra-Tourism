Rajgad Fort: The King of All Forts

Overview

Raigad Fort is one of the most important historical forts in India and is closely associated with Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, the founder of the Maratha Empire. Although the hill fort existed earlier and was known as Raigad Fort, it was captured by Shivaji Maharaj from the More dynasty in *1656. Recognizing its strategic location, he ordered the fort to be expanded and strengthened. The renowned architect Hiroji Indulkar designed and reconstructed the fort with massive stone walls, grand gateways, water reservoirs, palaces, and administrative buildings. Due to its strong natural defenses and commanding view of the surrounding valleys, Raigad became the ideal capital of the Maratha Empire.

Historical Highlights

Capital of Swarajya

Raigad Fort stands as one of the most significant and storied hill forts in Maharashtra. On 6 June 1674, this historic citadel witnessed the grand coronation of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, forever cementing its legacy as the birthplace of Hindavi Swarajya. Today, it remains an enduring symbol of Maratha courage, unyielding independence, and visionary good governance.

Interesting Facts

Raigad Fort served as the glorious capital of the Maratha Empire from 1674 to 1680. Visitors today can still retrace history by climbing its 1,700 stone steps to the summit, where thousands gather annually to honor Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj and experience the legacy of Hindavi Swarajya.

Coronation: A Historic Milestone

Raigad Fort stands as the majestic crown of the Maratha Empire. As the imperial capital where Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj was crowned in 1674, these hallowed heights radiate a timeless royal legacy of sovereignty, supreme courage, and visionary statecraft that continues to inspire generations. Shivaji Maharaj passed away at Raigad in April 1680, leaving behind an immortal legacy of valor.

Major Attractions

Attraction Description
Mahadarwaja The heavily fortified main entrance designed with strategic zig-zags to fend off invaders.
Palkhi Darwaja A specialized entrance reserved for royal palanquins of the Queen and royal family.
Rajyabhishek Bhashan Kaksh The royal coronation court featuring a grand replica of the throne.
Jagadishwar Temple A dedicated Shiva temple built by Shivaji Maharaj, housing his samadhi just outside.
Takmak Tok A sheer cliff drop point historically used for state executions.
Gangasagar Lake A large artificial water reservoir that supplied water to the fort.
Marketplace (Bazaar Peth) * An organized market where traders conducted business.
